What OSINTCompass Does

Research that brings order to chaos.

OSINTCompass provides structured research support using open-source information and public records. Whether you need to understand who is behind a website, map an entity's digital footprint, trace a paper trail, or organize evidence into a clear timeline — the goal is always the same: clarity from complexity.

How It Works

A clear process, every time.

Four defined stages from initial contact to final report — no guesswork, no surprises.

1
Step One

Intake

You describe your situation, what you need to understand, and what you already know. OSINTCompass scopes the research and confirms what is achievable.

2
Step Two

Research

Targeted investigation using public sources, open databases, archived records, and OSINT techniques relevant to your case. Methodical and documented throughout.

3
Step Three

Verification

Findings are cross-referenced against multiple sources before inclusion. Uncertain information is flagged clearly. Only what can be supported is reported.

4
Step Four

Report

A structured, plain-English report with sourced findings, a clear summary, and actionable conclusions — ready to share, submit, or act upon.

Ethics & Standards

Lawful. Ethical. Transparent.

OSINTCompass operates exclusively within the boundaries of legal, ethical, open-source research. The distinction matters and it is taken seriously.

What OSINTCompass does

  • Queries publicly available databases and registries
  • Uses open-source and public record sources only
  • Documents all sources for independent verification
  • Operates passively — no direct contact or interaction
  • Respects applicable privacy laws and data regulations
  • Clearly flags uncertain or unverified findings

What OSINTCompass does NOT do

  • Hacking or unauthorized system access
  • Bypassing access controls or authentication
  • Stalking, doxxing, or harassing individuals
  • Illegal surveillance or interception
  • Impersonation or social engineering
  • Research in support of harassment or illegal aims
